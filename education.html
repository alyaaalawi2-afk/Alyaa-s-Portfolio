📚 My Education Journey 📚

A Journey of Curiosity: From Learning Foundations to Exploring New Horizons

Education is a collection of memories, lessons, and experiences.

Each stage shaped my knowledge, personality, and the way I see the world today.

🧸

Kindergarten Years

The Beginning of Curiosity

The first step into the world of learning. This stage was about discovering new things, building friendships, learning basic communication, and developing curiosity through exploration and play.

📖

Primary School

Building Strong Foundations

Primary education introduced important foundations such as reading, writing, mathematics, teamwork, and discipline. It was a period of discovering interests, participating in activities, and learning the importance of responsibility.

🎒

Secondary School

Exploring Identity & Potential

Secondary school was a stage of growth and self-discovery. Besides academic learning, this period helped develop confidence, leadership, communication skills, and the ability to work with others.

🛠️

Diploma Journey

Learning Through Practical Knowledge

The diploma stage introduced a more specialised learning environment where theoretical knowledge was combined with practical applications. Through projects, assignments, and hands-on learning, valuable skills such as problem solving, technical thinking, and project management were developed.

🌱

Learning Beyond Formal Education

Workshops, Courses & Self Development

Education does not end after receiving a certificate. Joining workshops, attending skill programmes, and learning from different people and industries help expand knowledge beyond textbooks.

🎓

Current Academic Journey

Bachelor's Degree in Finance

Currently continuing my higher education at Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM). This chapter focuses on expanding knowledge in finance, business, management, and preparing for future opportunities.
